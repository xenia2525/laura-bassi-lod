01 Idea

The project starts from the English Wikipedia page Laura Bassi. Laura Bassi was an Italian physicist and, in 1732, the first woman to receive a university chair in Europe, at the University of Bologna.

Her life is made of clear relations: a patron who became pope, a husband who worked with her, students, correspondents, academies and dated appointments. The question behind the project is: how did a woman build a scientific career in eighteenth-century Bologna?

03 Theoretical model

The theoretical model shows how the 13 entities are related to Laura Bassi. Each node has its Wikidata identifier. Dashed lines are indirect relations, not written in the page (for example, that the university is located in Bologna).

04 Conceptual model

The theoretical model is then written as an ontology, reusing existing vocabularies. For three relations no existing term was found, so three new properties were created: patronOf, correspondedWith and precededBy.

  1. CIDOC CRMEvents and placescrm:E5_Event, crm:E53_Place, P11_had_participant, P7_took_place_at
  2. FOAF, schema.orgPeople and organisationsfoaf:Person, foaf:Organization, schema:spouse, schema:memberOf, schema:birthPlace

05 XML/TEI

The text of the Wikipedia page is encoded in TEI. The entities are marked in the text (persName, placeName, orgName, date, title, term) and described in dedicated lists: listPerson, listOrg, listPlace, listEvent, listBibl and listRelation. Each one is linked to its authority files (Wikidata, VIAF). Voltaire's quotation is encoded with q.

The text, annotated

<p><rs type="event" ref="#academy_election_1732">On
  <date when="1732-03-20">20 March 1732</date>, aged twenty,
  Bassi became the first woman elected to the
  <orgName ref="#academy_sciences_bologna">Academy of Sciences
  of the Institute of Bologna</orgName></rs>. …</p>

06 XML to HTML

An XSLT stylesheet transforms the TEI into HTML. It is run with a short Python script (lxml). In the HTML, every annotated entity becomes a link to its description.

tei2html.xsl (excerpt)

<xsl:template match="tei:body/tei:div/tei:head">
  <h2><xsl:apply-templates/></h2>
</xsl:template>
<xsl:template match="tei:div/tei:div/tei:head">
  <h3><xsl:apply-templates/></h3>
</xsl:template>
<xsl:template match="tei:p"><p><xsl:apply-templates/></p></xsl:template>

07 XML/TEI to RDF

A Python script (rdflib) reads the TEI and creates the RDF dataset, using the classes and properties of the conceptual model. Each relation in listRelation becomes a triple. The dataset is saved in Turtle.

laura_bassi.ttl (excerpt)

lb:laura_bassi a foaf:Person ;
    rdfs:label "Laura Bassi"@en ;
    schema:birthDate "1711-10-29"^^xsd:date ;
    schema:birthPlace lb:bologna ;
    schema:spouse lb:giuseppe_veratti ;
    rel:mentorOf lb:lazzaro_spallanzani ;
    lbo:correspondedWith lb:voltaire ;
    schema:memberOf lb:academy_sciences_bologna ;
    schema:knowsAbout lb:newtonianism ;
    owl:sameAs <http://www.wikidata.org/entity/Q541652>,
        <https://viaf.org/viaf/42647435> .

lb:thesis_defence_1732 a crm:E5_Event ;
    rdfs:label "Public defence of the forty-nine philosophical theses"@en ;
    dcterms:date "1732-04-17"^^xsd:date ;
    crm:P11_had_participant lb:laura_bassi ;
    crm:P14_carried_out_by lb:unibo ;
    crm:P7_took_place_at lb:sala_anziani .

tei2rdf.py laura_bassi.ttl
